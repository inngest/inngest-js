# Welcome!

Welcome to the Agents Tour.

It teaches `inngest/experimental/agents` one small idea at a time.
Every page has some code. Press **Run** and watch what it does.

The runs are real. They go to a Dev Server on your own machine, so the trace you see is the trace you would get in production.

Press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>]</kbd> or the Next button to go on.
