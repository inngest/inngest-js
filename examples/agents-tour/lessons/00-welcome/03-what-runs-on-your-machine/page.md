# What runs on your machine

Three things are running while you read this.

A **Dev Server**, the real Inngest one, which stores runs and shows their traces.
**Your code**, a small app that the Dev Server calls.
The **tour server**, which serves these pages and passes your edits along.

Press **Run** and the app restarts with your code. Nothing leaves your computer, and no API key is needed: the models in the tour are scripted.
